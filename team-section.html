The people behind the practice.

Dr. Mary Thamari

Director & Lead Consultant

Social scientist and senior evaluation and gender equality and social inclusion (GESI) specialist with 15+ years leading theory-based, mixed-methods evaluations across 20+ countries in Sub-Saharan Africa and South Asia, including fragile settings such as South Sudan, the DRC and Northern Ethiopia. She leads multi-country evaluations to OECD-DAC standards, using contribution analysis, outcome harvesting and Most Significant Change. Her work has been delivered for or funded by the EU, Irish Aid, Global Affairs Canada and the Gates and Mastercard Foundations. Formerly at Shell Foundation and Tearfund UK. PhD, University of Birmingham.

  • Evaluation
  • GESI
  • OECD-DAC
  • 20+ countries

Dr. Paul Ndungu

Senior Consultant

Market systems development (MSD) practitioner and evaluator with 15+ years across East Africa, designing and assessing programmes in agricultural value chains, youth entrepreneurship and organisational capability. His strength is systemic change analysis, using contribution analysis, outcome harvesting and process tracing. At Gatsby Africa (2018–2024) he built the MSD competency framework and led the Kenya Commercial Forestry Programme assessment. Earlier, at TechnoServe, he managed a Gates-funded outcomes measurement programme and led a youth entrepreneurship programme reaching 300,000 young people. DBA and MBA, USIU–Africa.

  • Market systems
  • Value chains
  • Youth enterprise
  • Systemic change

Dr. Farai Jena

Senior Consultant

Development economist with 15+ years in research, evaluation and programme management, and Visiting Professor of Economics at the University of Sussex Business School. Farai specialises in quantitative and mixed-methods analysis: designing indicators, targets and MEL frameworks, and analysing data by sex, age and social group to show who benefits and who is left behind. Clients include the World Bank, IFC, ILO, CGAP and the Commonwealth Secretariat, with work in Zimbabwe, Kenya and Ethiopia across agriculture, social protection, migration and health. Formerly Associate Dean at Sussex. PhD in Economics, Sussex.

  • Economics
  • MEL frameworks
  • Quantitative analysis
  • Social protection
